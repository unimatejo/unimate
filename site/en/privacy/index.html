Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What we collect

From your university account: your name, university email, photo, student number (if it's in the email), major, courses and sections. From you: posts, comments, stories, messages and photos you send, and your settings. Your location only if you turn on nearby friends or "Leave now", and we store the building, not exact coordinates.

Why we use it

To connect you with your university, organise your schedule and reminders, show you your course and major communities, and protect users from abuse and fraud. We do not sell your data to anyone or use it for any other purpose.

Signing in with Microsoft

If you sign in with your university Microsoft account, we get your name and email, and we import your account photo once, only if you have no photo (you can remove it any time and it will not come back). If you agree, we read the names of your Teams classes to add your courses and sections to your schedule; we do not read Teams messages or files.

Account type and academic title

Your university email decides whether your account is a student, faculty member or staff member. Nobody can type in an academic title (doctor, dean, head of department…) themselves: it comes from the official list the university gives us, or after review by UniMate administrators. The «University community» page only shows people who have created an account.

Study tools: absences, grades and GPA

The absences you record, and the grades, credit hours and GPA you enter, are private to you. Nobody else sees them: not your classmates, not your professors, not the university. We use them only to calculate your absences and GPA inside the app; the calculations are approximate and the university records are the official reference. They are permanently deleted when you delete your account.

«Studying now»

If you turn it on, only classmates enrolled in the same section see it, together with the place you wrote, if any. It switches off by itself at the time you chose, at most 6 hours, and is never shown to anyone you have a block with.

Who sees what

You control who sees your profile and posts in the privacy settings. Your Instagram and Snapchat usernames are shown only to the people you chose: everyone, friends, or close friends. Posts in university sections are shown to students of your university.

Official announcements from the university

Verified university staff (such as the dean's office, heads of department and authorised staff) can send official announcements to the students of their college or university, and you receive a notification for them. This feature does not let them see your private data or your messages.

UniMate admin access to your account and chats

During the current period, authorised UniMate administrators can view accounts and content on the platform, and can open private and group chats to run the platform, protect users, prevent fraud and abuse, handle complaints and provide support. Every time a chat is opened, who opened it and when is recorded in the admin log. The content of your chats is not given to any other party, including the university, unless a competent official authority requests it through a legal request. We may later limit this access to reported messages only; if so, we will update this policy and let you know.

Automatic protection from abuse

An automatic filter catches offensive words in posts, comments and messages; it works without anyone reading them. Someone who keeps sending abusive messages is muted from sending for periods that grow each time, and repeated cases are passed to administrators for review.

Screenshots

If you turn on screenshot blocking, we try to block it on the other person's device and tell you if someone tries. No system can stop someone filming the screen with another device, so keep that in mind.

Ads

In-app ads are from shops around the university and are labeled "Sponsored". We don't sell your data to anyone, and advertisers only see view and tap counts. If you turn on “Offers by email”, we send deals to your university email, with an unsubscribe link in every email.

How we protect your data

Your data is stored on Supabase servers and the storage services we use, and encrypted in transit. Every table has access rules so each person sees only what they are allowed to, and photos of private posts and stories open only for people allowed to see them. There are limits on the number of actions to stop spam and abuse, every admin action is logged, and there is a daily backup.

Vanishing messages and deleted content

Vanishing messages disappear from devices according to your setting, and are permanently deleted from our servers 30 days after they vanish, unless they are under an open report. When you delete a post, story or account, we keep a protected temporary copy that only the server can reach, for a limited time, so it can be restored if deleted by mistake or for safety and legal reasons. Messages are not included in this copy, and absence and grade data is deleted permanently.

Your rights

Under the Jordanian Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, you have the right to know what data we hold about you and get a copy, correct it, ask for it to be deleted, and object to its use. You can edit your data in Settings, ask for a copy or object from «Help & support», and delete your account from «Delete account» in Settings: it is deleted after 30 days, and if you sign in before then everything comes back. An account nobody signs in to for 12 months gets a notice and is deleted after 30 days, and its username becomes available to others.

Contact us

From the Help & support page inside the app, or at unimate.my/support.

Help and support

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In the app

Open Settings, then Help and support, choose a topic and describe the problem. We reply on the same page as soon as we can.

Can't sign in?

Make sure you use your official university email. If your university isn't supported yet, the app tells you and we add it soon.

Report a person or content

Tap ⋯ or Report on the post, profile or message. Reports are confidential.

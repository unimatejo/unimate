## Full description

### English
UniMate is the student app for Jordanian universities. Sign in with your university account and your world is ready:

- Timetable and reminders: your lectures and labs, with a reminder before each one and a "leave now" alert.
- Campus map: colleges, halls, library, cafeterias and gates, with walking time to your next lecture.
- Your university's community: summaries, assignments, tutoring, lost & found, market, book exchange, transport, events and opportunities.
- Groups for every course, major and college, joined automatically.
- Messages without phone numbers: requests from people you don't follow, disappearing messages, view-once photos and screenshot protection.
- Section swap and study buddy: find a classmate who wants the opposite section or wants to study the same course.
- Stories, posts, likes, comments, close friends and highlights.

Only students with a university email can join. Report and block are one tap away, and every report is reviewed.

## Age rating
17+ on the App Store (user-generated content and messaging between users), Teen on Google Play. Moderation, reporting and blocking are built in, as Apple's guideline 1.2 requires.

## Privacy (App Store "App Privacy" / Play "Data safety")
- Collected and linked to the user: name, university email, student number (from the email), photos and posts the user uploads, messages, approximate location (only if the user enables nearby friends / leave-now).
- Not sold, not used for tracking across other apps.
- In-app ads are from local businesses, labelled «ممول», and don't use personal data beyond university and college.
- Admins can open conversations when a report or complaint needs it; every access is logged (stated in the privacy policy inside the app).
